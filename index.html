The Spirit of MI: PACE

Four elements. None works alone. Take one away and the whole thing collapses into something else—persuasion, negotiation, or a lecture in disguise.

Select a pillar above to explore its meaning.

Partnership

You're working alongside your patient, not doing something to them. Two experts in the room: you on the clinical picture, they on their own life. Neither outranks the other.

Acceptance

You respect your patient's right to choose, including the choice not to change today. Acceptance isn't approval of the behaviour. It's respect for the person holding it.

Compassion

You act in your patient's best interest, not for your own convenience, your own comfort, or to end an awkward consultation faster. Compassion keeps the other three honest.

Evocation

The Focus

You draw the patient's own motivations, values, and reasons out, rather than installing yours. It's the most counterintuitive of the four, and the one that does the most work. We'll spend the next page on this alone.
